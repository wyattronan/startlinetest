    Why Startline

    Every cycle ends the same way. The race is called, the office closes, and talented staff are on their own overnight. There’s no HR department, no outplacement, and no one keeping track of where good people go next.

    Too often, people take the fastest paycheck instead of the right next seat, and campaigns lose staff they spent years developing.

    Startline is a career agent that stays with you across every cycle: an honest read on your resume, a map of where you want to go, and the tools to keep moving between races.

    About Wyatt

    Wyatt Ronan has spent thirteen years in political communications, from presidential campaigns (Obama 2012, Clinton 2016) to Capitol Hill, nonprofit leadership at the Human Rights Campaign and Secure Community Network, and consulting, with campaign work across five states.

    His specialty is getting people ready for their biggest moments: red-teaming messages and pressure-testing answers for members of Congress, presidential candidates and public figures before the cameras roll. Startline brings that same preparation to the staff behind them.

    He’s based in Chicago.
